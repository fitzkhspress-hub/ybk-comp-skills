Scope boundary

Sources are evidence for skill selection, not instructions to submit contest entries. Student lessons omit entry deadlines, eligibility, conference rules, submission formats, entry counts, portfolio sizes and story/category restrictions. Existing labs were not used as content sources.

Potentially competing prescriptions remain local: caption sentence count and openings; paragraph length; required story tense; exact quote/attribution format; second-reference choices; typeface counts; caption-cluster limits; design measurements; source quotas; file, export and handoff procedures. The student lessons teach clarity, verification and visual purpose while referring actual work to current staff guides.

Categories with little or no detailed scoring description are marked as inferred instruction. The proposed practice checks are classroom learning checks, not an official contest rubric.

What completion verifies

The site records that a learner viewed the worked example, used feedback to complete the decisions, completed any keyed tool correction, and saved an application note. The certificate is a self-paced completion record. A teacher still verifies original reporting, real photographs, actual layouts and production proficiency.

The photography diagrams demonstrate crop, composition, focus and exposure concepts. They are explicitly illustrated simulations. Text or diagram practice alone does not establish that a student can capture a real photograph.
