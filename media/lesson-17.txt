A storytelling moment reveals an action or relationship worth seeing. It also needs technical usability: the important subject should be in focus, exposure should preserve meaningful detail and color should be credible. Technical polish alone does not supply a story.

How to do it.
Step 1. Anticipate the real action and watch how the people interact.
Step 2. Check focus on the important subject and exposure for meaningful highlights and shadows.
Step 3. Review both the moment and the technical result; use the approved camera workflow for settings.

Worked example.
