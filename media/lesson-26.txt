Final review checks relationships across the actual artifact: names against records, captions against images, graphics against sources and type against the final layout. A repair is complete only after you review the corrected output under your current production workflow.

How to do it.
Step 1. Use the approved staff checklist to inspect the actual final page or export.
Step 2. Record a specific problem, the source that establishes it and the repair needed.
Step 3. Check the new output after the change, including links, references and information affected elsewhere.

Worked example.
Find independent errors. Roster: Maya Chen. Caption: Mya Chen. Data source: 18. Graphic: 81. The name and value need separate corrections. The polished layout does not make either accurate.
Check the relationships. The corrected caption still sits beside the wrong illustration. Proofing the words alone misses the image-to-caption connection. Inspect the whole artifact.
Close the repair loop. Source → correction → new output → recheck. Use the staff’s final review, export and handoff procedures. A box checked in this activity does not substitute for a verified production artifact.

Watch for this. Do not assume an edit in the working file is present in the export you are about to hand off.
